Payroll & Salary Screening01

WageLens

A guided payroll review, with evidence, next steps and your data kept on your device.

About this project: WageLens

See every pay period clearly

A browser-based Skilled Worker payroll screening tool with local occupation suggestions from actual job duties. Payroll stays on your device; potential findings and code suggestions need human review.

  • Excel, CSV, TSV and digital PDF input
  • Research preview: findings need human review
  • Paste advert links for reference; duties are entered manually for matching
News & Media02

BiasCheck

See political framing through article quotes and sourced context.

About this project: BiasCheck

News framing, inspected

Read the news, then check the bias. It surfaces political framing using exact article quotes and sourced reporter context, so you can judge the wording yourself.

  • Quotes from the article, not summaries of it
  • Reporter context with sources
Discovery & Opportunities03

Open Atlas

Discover conferences, fellowships and research opportunities.

About this project: Open Atlas

Conferences, fellowships, research

Source-backed opportunities from around the world, with filters for type, region, free access and funding. It points you to official pages and keeps unknowns visible.

  • 21 academic sources in a browsable directory
  • Daily discovery and a saved shortlist

A free optional-subject planner for UPSC preparation.

About this project: Mainspring

Your optional. Your next move.

A free UPSC optional-subject planner that turns self-assessed syllabus gaps into a study plan, with writing and revision built in.

  • Progress saved on your device
Careers & AI Tools05

RoleMint

Tailor your resume to a role, using your own API key.

About this project: RoleMint

Your experience. Ready for this role.

Tailor a resume to a job description using your own Groq or Gemini API key. Review the draft and a before-and-after keyword report, then download your focused resume.

  • PDF and Word input / No account needed
  • Uses existing resume facts; review before applying
