The same habit, at work

Open Atlas states this rule on its own page. It’s the habit I bring to analyst work: trace the number to its source, say what the data can’t tell you, and don’t fill gaps with guesses.

In supply chain work that meant mapping where inventory data came from and tracing why systems disagreed before anyone argued about the totals.
